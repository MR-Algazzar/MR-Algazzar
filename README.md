### 📌 How I Work

- **Data structure first:** Validating sources, fixing inconsistencies, and modeling relationships before building visuals.
- **Decision-focused reporting:** Designing layouts around specific operational and business questions rather than decorative charts.
- **Reproducible workflows:** Documenting steps, queries, and transformations so analyses can be audited and updated cleanly.
